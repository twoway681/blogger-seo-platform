# Installing a template on your Blogger blog

1. Download the `.xml` file.
2. On blogger.com open your blog, then **Theme** in the left menu.
3. Click the arrow next to **Customize** and choose **Restore**.
4. Upload the `.xml` file. Blogger applies it immediately.
5. Optional but recommended: before restoring, use **Backup** in the same menu to
   save your current theme so you can roll back.

After switching, re-check **Settings > Crawlers and indexing**: keep "Enable custom
robots.txt" off unless you know you need it - a bad robots.txt is one of the
indexing killers this platform exists to fix.
